# Bento
A tiny sushi game!

## Game Guide
The legendary Tadashi dynasty have ruled for nearly 314 years. Everyone in the kingdom is content with the Tadashis' rule - except for the Noeru.  
For the past thirteen years, the Noeru have tried - and failed - to overthrow the Tadashis. No one knows why the Noeru are so unhappy with the Tadashis, but people do know that the Noeru's main goal is to weaken the Tadashis with poison. Like everyone in the kingdom, the Tadahis eat plenty of sushi! Many Noeru work at sushi shops, slipping poison into Tadashi sushi orders. They must be careful not to poison fellow Noeru.  
In your childhood, you helped with your family's sushi shop. Now, you will join the royal guard and search for Noeru to arrest at sushi shops.  
When you enter the shop, a questionable shopkeeper will ask for your order. You can order sushi using the buttons at the bottom of the game. Then, after consuming your sushi, you will either be safe or be poisoned by a Noeru. Your goal is to catch as many Noeru as possible and validate as many loyal shopkeepers as possible before losing all three lives. There is a 44/314 chance of being poisoned by a Noeru.

## Contributions / For Developers
This game is unfinished. The original developer has no intention of finishing this game. Frontend developers may build derivatives of this game for personal, educational, and/or commercial use.  
There were plans for an additional minigame under [this path](play/poisoner). This minigame remains unfinished. It was meant to be a minigame where, instead of serving the Tadashis and catching Noeru, the player serves the Noeru and poisons Tadashis. All assets are fully completed, but code must be updated and changed.
