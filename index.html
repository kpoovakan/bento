Bento

silly little JavaScript game by kpoovakan




the legendary Tadashi dynasty have ruled for nearly three hundred and fourteen years. everyone is content with the Tadashis' rule - except for the Noeru.

for the past thirteen years, the Noeru have tried - and failed - to overthrow the Tadashis. no one knows why the noeru are so unhappy with the Tadashis.

the Noeru's main goal is to weaken the Tadashis with posion. like everyone in the kingdom, the Tadashis eat plenty of sushi! many Noeru work at sushi shops, slipping posion into Tadashi sushi orders. they must be careful not to poison fellow Noeru.

in your childhood, you helped with your family's sushi shop. now you are old enough for hire. you can join the royal guard and search for Noeru to arrest at sushi shops. or, you can join the Noeru and poison the Tadashis' sushi orders.
