Bento game guide

play gamereturn to homepage

when you enter the shop, a questionable shopkeeper will ask for your order. you can order sushi using the buttons at the bottom of the game. then, after consuming your sushi, you will either be safe or be poisoned by a Noeru. your goal is to catch as many Noeru as possible and validate as many loyal shopkeepers as possible before losing all three lives. there is a 44 out of 314 chance of being poisoned by a Noeru.
